I'm Kateryna — a Product Designer based in Barcelona with 4+ years of experience in mobile apps, EdTech, and SaaS products. I lead design end-to-end: from UX research and user interviews through to final UI and component systems in Figma.

I specialise in products where complexity needs to be untangled — content-heavy platforms, multi-step flows, dashboards, and 0→1 builds. I think in systems, document the edge cases others skip, and care about the decisions that make a product feel trustworthy, not just polished.

Currently the Lead Product Designer at DanceAway.app — a pre-launch mobile app for the dance community — while taking select freelance projects on the side.
