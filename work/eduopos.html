Eduopos — Education Platform

End-to-end UX/UI for an online education platform — structuring courses and learning flows so students always know where they are and what to do next.

Role UX/UI Designer
Type Freelance · Upwork
Platform Web
Tools Figma · FigJam

Overview

The challenge

Eduopos is an online education platform. Learning products easily become cluttered — courses, lessons, progress and settings all compete for attention. The goal was an interface that keeps the focus on learning and makes the next step obvious.

"Students should always know where they are and what to do next."

Course page

Process

Structure & flows

I mapped the core journeys — finding a course, moving through lessons, and tracking progress — and organised the information architecture around them, so each screen supports one clear task.

UI design

A calm, consistent visual system with clear hierarchy: content first, navigation predictable, and progress always visible.
