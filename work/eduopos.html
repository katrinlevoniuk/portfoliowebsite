Product deep dive

Onboarding & personalisation Redesign

María and Carlos move through the same onboarding, but the system adapts intent, region and specialty into personalized materials.

MMaría’s moment

Signs up, picks Andalucía and Primaria, and every next screen already speaks her curriculum.

The problem. Each autonomous community and specialty has its own temario. Onboarding had to capture both clearly before the user sees any content.

01
A first screen that explains the value.
Splash, logo and Get Started set up “Estudia con método” before sign-in.
02
Intent first.
Users choose why they are here (temario, flashcards, supuestos or planning), so the experience starts from their goal.
03
Especialidad and Comunidad Autónoma.
Selected states, descriptions, card sizes, spacing and CTA refined so the choice is easy to make and to check.
04
One icon style.
Specialty and region icons aligned in stroke, visual weight and detail into a single set.
SIGN UP → VERIFY → INTENT → REGION → SPECIALTY
04.2

Product deep dive

Fichas / worksheet generator Redesign

An AI worksheet generator for teachers. I audited the existing flow and explored how to extend it beyond AI generation.

MMaría’s moment

Needs a maths worksheet for tomorrow’s class: browses the catalog, sets type and filters, generates.

The problem. Teachers need ready materials fast: Matemáticas, Lectura, Crucigrama, Sopa de letras. Materials they already had stayed outside the app.

01
Audit of the existing flow.
Catalog, activity type, filters, parameters and result reviewed step by step.
02
Parameters before generation.
Teachers set type, level and layout before generating, with credits always visible.
03
Upload existing document.
Importing materials teachers already own: a different value from AI generation.
04
Scan document.
Digitising paper materials, explored as a separate path, not just another button.
CATALOG → TYPE → FILTERS → CONFIGURE → GENERATE
04.3

Product deep dive

Supuestos prácticos New

Practice cases with nested phases, sections and references, restructured to be read and studied on a phone.

CCarlos’s moment

Creates a practice case manually or imports a PDF, then studies it phase by phase.

The problem. A case goes Explicación → Índice → Resultado → Estudio, with Fase Inicial / Procesual / Final nested inside. Tab navigation couldn’t hold that depth.

01
Índice instead of tabs.
A table of contents handles nested phases and sections; users jump straight to the part they need.
02
Resultado as the core.
Resultado is the main content of a case; Explicación and Estudio support it.
03
Scannable long text.
Clear hierarchy for phases, sections and references, so long cases don’t lose the reader.
04
Create or import.
Cases can be written step by step or imported from an existing PDF.
LIBRARY → CREATE / IMPORT → REVIEW → PRACTICE → RESULT
04.4

Product deep dive

Temario & study loop New module

Temario, fragments, statuses, notes and progress: the daily study loop for opositores.

CCarlos’s moment

Chooses a topic and continues reading from his saved position, marking fragments as he goes.

The problem. Topics hold dozens or hundreds of fragments in a fixed order. Opening each one separately would make studying slow, and progress could easily repeat across screens.

01
Temario → Tema → Section → Fragment.
One hierarchy that keeps the original order and shows where you are.
02
Open or collapsible.
Content stays open where reading flows and collapses where it doesn’t, so fragments aren’t opened one by one.
03
Memorizado / Necesita práctica.
Status lives on the fragment itself, so progress isn’t repeated elsewhere.
04
Notes and practice.
Highlights become notes; flashcards and the progress map close the loop.
FLASHCARDS → TEMARIO → NOTES → FAVORITES → PROGRESS
