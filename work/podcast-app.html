01 — The Idea

Bringing podcasts
together with reading

Podreads is an innovative app that changes how users interact with podcasts. Unlike competitors, it lets you both listen to and read your favorite shows — enhancing the experience for different contexts and needs.

Whether you prefer listening on the go or reading, Podreads offers a flexible way to enjoy podcasts like never before.

03 — UX Research

Survey:
8 participants,
real answers

I aimed to gather insights into user habits, preferences, and pain points related to podcast listening — asking what people actually use today and what they're missing.

“It seems like having live podcasts would be a great feature, but also having the ability to save and replay them would be even better. Maybe even allowing listeners to ask questions during the live session would make the experience more interactive.”

“I personally haven't found any good podcast apps, so I just listen to them on Telegram or watch them on YouTube if I have premium. To me, podcasts are just like short audiobooks.”

“Other podcast apps I've tried are okay, but they all seem to have limited topics. I usually just create a “podcasts” folder in Telegram and add channels that cover topics I'm interested in, like history, space, and motivation. It's all there, and it's free.”

“Sometimes, it's hard to understand what the speaker is saying, so having the option to read the text while listening would be helpful.”

“Being able to save specific parts of the audio or video would be great for future reference.”

What the survey told me

Podcasts are often background listening — so the app shouldn't demand attention, but should make it easy to catch up on what you missed.

04 — Strategy

The goal statement

A podcast app will allow users to discover and listen to podcasts tailored to their specific interests, which will positively affect them by enhancing their knowledge, personal growth, and entertainment experience.

The goal is to create a user-centred podcast app that caters to diverse user needs. Through personalised features and efficient content organisation, we aim to enhance user satisfaction. Success will be measured by positive user feedback reflecting their satisfaction with the app's content and features.

1 Content Organization

Busy users need
to find content fast.

Alex is a busy marketing specialist who needs a podcast app that offers efficient content organisation because of limited time.

User moment

On the metro to work, finds a great episode and needs to file it for later in seconds.

Solution: Intuitive episode management — add episodes to playlists directly from the player view. Users can create collections or choose existing ones on the spot.

The episode menu puts Add to playlist one tap away from the player.

Problem solved for Alex →

Pick an existing playlist or create a new one without leaving the episode.

Problem solved for Alex ←
2 Language Barrier

Understanding spoken
content across languages.

Emily is a student who needs a podcast app with subtitles because the language barrier sometimes makes it difficult to understand spoken content.

User moment

Listening to an English podcast, misses a sentence and wants to read it instead of rewinding.

Solution: A convenient toggle switch lets users enable or disable subtitles. A word-highlighting feature visually links spoken words to their written counterparts in real time.

One toggle switches the player between listening and reading.

Problem solved for Emily ←

The transcript shows timestamps and a language picker, and highlights the words being spoken.

Problem solved for Emily ↓
3 Manage Subscription

Following content
that actually matters.

Sarah is a self-development person who needs a podcast app that makes it easy to manage subscriptions because it helps her find content faster.

User moment

Opens the app with ten minutes to spare and wants only the shows she follows.

Solution: A switcher between "All Podcasts" and "Following" lets users segment content by preference. The "Following" section organises topics alphabetically for effortless navigation.

The All / Following switch separates discovery from shows you already follow.

Following is sorted alphabetically, with an A–Z index for quick jumps.

Problem solved for Sarah →
4 Effortless Segment Saving

Save the moments
that matter most.

Ed is a writer who needs a podcast app with easy-to-save segments because he wants to capture important information he often misses while listening.

User moment

Hears a line worth quoting while walking, but can't stop to write it down.

Solution: Users can save specific segments, add written notes with custom font styling, and filter saved content for easy retrieval — without ever pausing the podcast.

Saved episodes live in playlists, with a filter for quick retrieval.

Problem solved for Ed ←

Copied passages become notes that keep the segment’s timestamp, with simple text formatting.

Problem solved for Ed →
5 Personalized Recommendations

Content tailored
to your interests.

Michael is a back-end developer who needs a podcast app with personalised recommendations because of a lack of content diversity in existing apps.

User moment

Has heard everything his usual shows put out and wants something new that still fits his interests.

Solution: "Trending Podcasts" and "Top Podcasts" sections personalised by user preferences. A dedicated section for live and real-time broadcasts.

Home opens with Live podcasts, then Playlists for you and Trending podcasts.

Problem solved for Michael ↓
6 Real-Time Interaction

Engage live with
hosts and listeners.

Maria is a podcast enthusiast who needs real-time interaction during live podcast sessions because she wants to engage with hosts as the podcast unfolds.

User moment

Her favourite host goes live tonight, and she has a question ready.

Solution: A live chat feature lets users ask questions, share opinions, and interact in real time. Users can also join before a live session to set reminders and prepare questions.

Before a live session: event details, speakers and a Join button.

Problem solved for Maria ←

During the session, the Comments tab becomes a live chat with hosts and listeners.

Problem solved for Maria →
