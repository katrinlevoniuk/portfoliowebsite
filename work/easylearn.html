Overview

The challenge

Design a podcast app that makes content discovery feel natural and subscription management effortless — across both iOS and Android, without sacrificing the distinct feel of each platform.

The key tension: podcast apps are deeply personal. Users have habits, queues, and subscriptions they've built up over years. The interface needed to respect existing mental models while improving on common pain points in competitor apps.

Process

Research

Competitive analysis of Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Pocket Casts, and Overcast. Identified key differentiators and common drop-off points in onboarding, discovery, and player interactions.

IA & user flows

Mapped the core user journeys: discovering a new podcast, subscribing and getting notified, and consuming episodes in different contexts (background, commute, focused listening). Each flow was designed to minimise steps to the core action.

UI design

Designed 50+ screens including onboarding, home feed, search, podcast detail, episode list, player (mini + full), queue management, subscription settings, and profile. Both dark and light themes.
